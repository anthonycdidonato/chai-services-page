Education as a Service

CHAI works directly with organizations to better equip their colleagues on the foundational components of responsible AI. Our programs translate the CHAI framework into practical skills your teams can apply on day one — delivered by the people who helped write it.

  • AI Intake and Procurement

    Build a repeatable front door for AI: structured intake, evidence requirements, vendor due diligence, and contracting considerations aligned with CHAI assurance standards.

  • Risk-tiered Governance

    Operationalize the CHAI Risk Categorization Tool: classify solutions across patient-safety and technology modifiers, match oversight to risk level, and map mitigations to governance controls.

  • Model Evaluation

    Plan and interpret local validation: performance, fairness, and robustness testing, monitoring for drift, and evidence review for both predictive and generative AI.

  • Applied Model Cards and Transparency

    Read, request, and produce Applied Model Cards so clinical and operational teams can understand what an AI solution does, for whom, and with what limitations.

  • Responsible AI Foundations for Leaders

    Executive and board-level briefings on the AI lifecycle, accountability structures, regulatory expectations, and the questions leaders should be asking.

  • Custom Curriculum

    Role-based programs tailored to your organization — from frontline clinicians and informaticists to procurement, compliance, and data science teams.

Insights

Insights

Our Insights offering is designed for leaders who want to stay at the forefront of healthcare innovation through ongoing access to CHAI data, insights, and connections. Available to CHAI members and, for an annual fee, to non-member organizations.

  • Proprietary Data

    Aggregated, de-identified views from the CHAI Registry and assurance programs: which solutions are being deployed, at what risk tier, and with what evidence.

  • Market Intelligence

    Quarterly briefings on the health AI landscape — vendor movements, regulatory and policy developments, and emerging use cases — distilled for executive decision-making.

  • Community Insights

    Benchmarks and lessons learned from peer health systems, payers, and developers: how others are governing, evaluating, and scaling AI — and where they are getting stuck.

  • Guided Member Experience

    A dedicated CHAI point of contact, curated introductions across the community, and priority access to working groups, symposia, and new resources.

Who it is for: CHAI members receive Insights as part of their membership tier. Non-member organizations can subscribe for an annual fee. Pricing will be set based on the interest we hear from this page.

How an engagement works

Light-touch to start. We scope every engagement around your organization's priorities and existing governance maturity.

1

Share your priorities

Submit a short interest form. A member of the CHAI team will follow up within a few business days to understand your goals, audience, and timeline.

2

Scope the program

Together we select the right offerings and format — a single workshop, a multi-session course, or an ongoing Insights subscription — and agree on outcomes.

3

Deliver and measure

CHAI experts deliver the program, provide supporting resources, and help you track adoption against the CHAI framework.
