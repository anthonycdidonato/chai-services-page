Education as a Service

CHAI works directly with organizations to better equip their colleagues on the foundational components of responsible AI. Our programs translate the CHAI framework into practical skills your teams can apply on day one — delivered by the people who helped write it.

  • AI Intake and Procurement

    Build a repeatable front door for AI: structured intake, evidence requirements, vendor due diligence, and contracting considerations aligned with CHAI assurance standards.

  • Risk-tiered Governance

    Operationalize the CHAI Risk Categorization Tool: classify solutions across patient-safety and technology modifiers, match oversight to risk level, and map mitigations to governance controls.

  • Model Evaluation

    Plan and interpret local validation: performance, fairness, and robustness testing, monitoring for drift, and evidence review for both predictive and generative AI.

  • AI Governance

    Stand up or strengthen a responsible AI governance program through a four-session webinar series or a four-hour executive workshop, virtual or on-site. Modules cover AI intake and transparency, risk and mitigations, governance controls (AI policy, committee design, roles, and resourcing), and organizational processes (lifecycle, data, and third-party management), applied to your organization's own use cases.

  • Responsible AI Foundations for Leaders

    Executive and board-level briefings on the AI lifecycle, accountability structures, regulatory expectations, and the questions leaders should be asking.

  • Custom Curriculum

    Role-based programs tailored to your organization — from frontline clinicians and informaticists to procurement, compliance, and data science teams.

Insights

Insights

Our Insights offering is designed for leaders who want to stay at the forefront of healthcare innovation through ongoing access to CHAI data, insights, and connections. Available to CHAI members and, for a fee, to non-member organizations.

  • Proprietary Data

    Aggregated, de-identified views from the CHAI Registry, working group insight reports, and shared feedback.

  • Market Intelligence

    Briefings on the health AI landscape (e.g., vendor movements, regulatory and policy developments, and emerging use cases — distilled for executive decision-making).

  • Community Insights

    Benchmarks and lessons learned from peer health systems, payers, and developers: how others are governing, evaluating, and scaling AI — and where they are getting stuck.
